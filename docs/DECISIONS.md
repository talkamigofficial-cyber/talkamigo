## 25 Nov 2026 — full audit (4 testers: chats, rooms/teacher, Flicks/Me/money/settings, sign-up/Connect) — part 1 fixes
- **Me numbers are real now** (founder's bug): every correction you send (chats and Flick comments) and every minute in a call, talk room or live is logged per day and language (`S.act`). This week (spoken, corrections), Today x / goal, the streak, the day dots (never future days) and the time under each language on the Language card are worked out from it. The demo account has a believable 27-day history; new accounts start at 0.
- **Correction sheet + keyboard** (founder's black screen): the sheet now rides above the keyboard like Flick comments; on small phones it scrolls to the box you type in. "Correction sent" confirms; corrections carry a time; no English "Corrected by …" filler.
- **Blocking really blocks**: you leave their chat/settings, can't message/call/gift them (clear message), they're unfollowed both ways, removed from close friends, follower/following lists and search (people, Flicks, rooms). Unblocking brings the chat back.
- **Muting a chat** (swipe or Chat settings — one switch now) stops its banners. **Hide their Flicks** hides them. **Allow calls off** blocks your own calls to them too. **Pin to top** puts the chat above groups, with a pin.
- **Privacy settings applied**: "Who can send me gifts: People I follow" and "Who can message me" (message requests) now work. Muted people are left out of "In a room now".
- **Accounts**: an unfinished sign-up is no longer offered as an account to log into; logging in with a method that has no account here offers to create one (no more landing in the demo account); a new account starts clean (promise unticked, no old search, no demo message requests). Usernames: reserved names refused, 20 characters max, not taken by another account on the phone, change once every 30 days.
- **Money**: Plus shows its real renewal date (from the day you bought it); cash-out only from Diamonds past the 14-day hold and 2 a month; Diamonds→coins only from available Diamonds; buying Infinity while on Plus reminds you to cancel Plus; frames ask for a second tap before spending coins.
- **Flicks**: a new Flick uses the language and level you picked; the likes/comment you're told about after posting really appear on it, and tapping opens it. Automatic translation asks for AI consent first.
- **Other**: Duo invites wait for a yes; waves have a real time; calls leave a line in the chat ("📞 Voice call · 3:12") and the Duo day goes up at most once a day; polls' answer boxes are full height; the press-and-hold menu stays open; "1 friend"; tour Coins card works in every language; French no longer guesses gender on Welcome and the promise; ~25 texts translated.
- **Lessons work end to end (25 Nov audit)**: a planned lesson becomes a real room at its time — students who reserved see "Join the lesson" (from 5 min before to the end), the teacher sees "Start your lesson" and hosts it. Reminders come at the real time (10 min before, and at the start), not 9 s after reserving. Teachers can't plan a lesson in the past; their own lessons always show in Schedule.
- **Teacher asked to fix something** (new ID photo / payouts) goes back to their normal plan until approved again (no Infinity, no "No limit"). The application message no longer promises approval "in a few seconds".
- **Room/live extras**: "X is live" alerts come once per room (not on repeat); the Join-live sheet says the camera stays off unless you turn it on; pinning a notice later in Room settings completes the room task; demo gifts in your own room/live only arrive with an audience (5+), and every received gift's Diamonds wait the 14-day hold (they can't be cashed out at once); the minimised room card steps aside on the Open room screen.

## 26 Nov 2026 — Connect → Nearby shows people who are really near
- Bug (founder): Sam in London saw Pablo in Mexico City first, "≈ 0 km". Most cities had no map position, so the distance fell back to your own position (0 km) and those people went to the top; Nearby also never left anyone out.
- Now: every city has a position (capital of the country as a fallback, never 0 km by mistake), the distance is measured from your own city, and Nearby only lists people within 500 km (`NEAR_KM`; app_config in the real app). For London: Noah (Bristol 170 km), Léa and Hugo (Brussels 320 km), Marc (Paris 340 km), James (Dublin 460 km).
- City and distance sit on their own line under "Online now" (French wrapped badly). If no one is close: "No one near you yet · Only people within about 500 km show here · See everyone". 8 languages.
